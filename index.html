06 / FAQ

Before you
set out

The essential details for your first step into Midgard.

Is Ragnarok: The New World free to play?+

Yes. Steam lists the game as free to play with in-game purchases.

Can I play on PC?+

Yes. The game is available on PC through Steam. A Gravity account is required through the game launcher.

How much storage and memory do I need?+

Steam lists 30 GB of available storage, 12 GB RAM minimum, and 16 GB RAM recommended.

Which languages does Steam list?+

Steam lists English, Indonesian, Thai, and Simplified Chinese for interface, full audio, and subtitles.
