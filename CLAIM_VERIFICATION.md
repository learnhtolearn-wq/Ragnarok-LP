## Claims safe to publish

- Explore an open-world Ragnarok adventure in Midgard.
- Steam currently lists eight classes, including Druid. Keep the Steam scope because official platform listings contain an older seven-class reference.
- Druid is described on Steam as having Werewolf, Wereraptor, and Human Arcanist forms.
- Defeat MVPs and use them as mounts.
- Customize 100+ looks, including styling and dye options.
- Trade gear through official trading, auction, or stall systems.
- Team up with other players for party and guild content.
- Play free on Steam, with in-game purchases.
- A Gravity account is required through the game launcher.
- 30 GB storage, 12 GB minimum RAM, and 16 GB recommended RAM.
- Steam lists English, Indonesian, Thai, and Simplified Chinese support across interface, full audio, and subtitles.

## Claims to remove or revise before publication

- Remove any complete list of all eight class names until an official current list is supplied.
- Revise “eight classes” to the Steam-scoped wording above, or obtain publisher confirmation that resolves the official seven versus eight class copy conflict.
- Do not publish OS, CPU, or GPU requirements because Steam leaves those fields blank.
- Use “100+ looks,” not “100+ appearance options,” to match the official Steam wording.
- Avoid promising a specific frame rate for every PC. If used, say “supports up to 120 FPS” and qualify it by hardware.
- Avoid broad claims such as “all builds,” “every role,” or “no meta build.” Use the narrower official wording about flexible skill combinations.
- Do not imply that Steam availability grants commercial reuse rights for artwork, logos, or video.
